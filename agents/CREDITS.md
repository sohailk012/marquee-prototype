# Credits

The specialist know-how in `knowledge.js` was distilled from the open-source libraries below, rewritten in our own words, and adapted to a UK private dental practice (patients, consultations, Invisalign, implants, whitening, nervous patients) and to UK rules (CAP Code and ASA, GDC guidance on advertising, prescription-only medicines, PECR and UK GDPR). No passages were copied at length. Each skill in `knowledge.js` lists its `sources`, keyed to `SOURCES` in the same file.

Licences were checked on 6 November 2026 from each repository's LICENSE file on its default branch. All four are MIT, which allows adaptation provided the copyright notice and permission notice are included. They are reproduced in full below.

## 1. Marketing Skills for AI agents

- **Author:** Corey Haines
- **URL:** https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills
- **Licence:** MIT
- **What we adapted:** the main source. Techniques from the `copywriting`, `copy-editing` (seven sweeps), `ads` (budget split, 20% scaling steps, weekly operating check with hold/fix/test/investigate, brand vs non-brand, landing page headline matching, bidding progression), `ad-creative` (headline rules, testable hypotheses, Google character limits), `social` (content pillars, hook types, short-video structure and caption rules, repurposing, metrics that matter), `emails` (one job per email, nurture sequence order and timing, subject lines), `sms` (sender ID, one link, 160-character segments, consent), `referrals` (trigger moments, two-sided rewards), `events` (most value before and after the event, 48-hour follow-up), `revops` (lead scoring with fit, engagement and negative signals, speed to lead, routing, lifecycle stages, deduplication), `attribution` (source of truth, never summing platforms, first-touch vs last-touch, self-reported "how did you hear"), `conversion-tracking` (fire on confirmed success, a tag firing is not a conversion arriving, observed/reported/inferred), `cro` (form fields and button copy), `customer-research` (voice of the customer, profiles from evidence), `competitors`, `marketing-psychology` (constraints, 80/20, second-order thinking) and `offers` skills. The `copywriting` skill credits part of its method to Profound's `skills-marketing-library` (MIT); we used it only as re-expressed in Corey Haines' skill.

## 5. Marquee (our own work)

Source id `marquee`. Our own UK dental and compliance notes: the Oakmere voice, nervous-patient handling, treatment economics, open evenings, catchment, and the checks based on the CAP Code and ASA approach to health claims, GDC guidance on advertising (titles, specialist lists, website information), the ban on advertising prescription-only medicines such as botulinum toxin, tooth whitening rules, testimonials and fake reviews, prices and consumer credit wording, Google and Meta health ad policies, PECR, UK GDPR special category data, patient photo consent and cookie consent. These are checks for a prototype, not legal advice; a real deployment should have them reviewed by a compliance professional.
